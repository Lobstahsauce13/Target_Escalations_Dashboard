Status & priority guide

Overdue: the SLA deadline (column AI, or B if AI is empty) has passed and the escalation is not done.

Done: it has a Best Buy resolved date (AF), the tracker status (BM) is Completed, or the Best Buy status (AG) is CLOSED.

Due soon: the deadline is within the next 48 hours.

P1–P4: priority from column AB. P1 means immediate follow-up within 5 days, P2 follow-up in 7 days, P3 waiting on parts, P4 issue not provided.

Next step: who owns the next action (column A).

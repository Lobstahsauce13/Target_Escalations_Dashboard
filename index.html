Target Escalation Dashboard

Status guide

New: Maximo status APPR, a new escalation.

Open: VDECWO, a work order that is still open.

Completed: VINPRG, a work order that has been completed.

Closed, not removed: VDECWC, a work order that has been closed but not removed.

Past due: New or Open, and its LOS Finish date has passed. Due within 48 hours: its LOS Finish is in the next 48 hours.

Only PM work orders are loaded. CM rows in the Data file are skipped.
